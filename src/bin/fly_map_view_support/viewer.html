Fly connectome, brain activity, and measured body

A standalone diagnostic view. Network nodes and edges are deterministic samples; voltage, spikes, sensors, actions, position, and heading are measured values.

Brain: connectome flow and sampled activation

Rows are presynaptic cell types; columns are postsynaptic cell types. Brightness is the logarithmic count of real map edges among the 48 most connected types.

Horizontal placement groups sampled neurons by cell type; it is not anatomy. Green edges are excitatory, red edges inhibitory. With a matching trace, node color shows measured membrane voltage and larger bright nodes show measured spikes.

Body: measured position, sensors, and actions

Blue marks every being at the selected sample. Yellow marks the selected being, its forward vector, and only its measured trail up to the current sample. The x/z axes use one equal scale and exclude any synthetic origin.

Coverage and asymmetry are ratios; steer, throttle, and brake are commands; speed is metres per second. Signed channels keep a visible zero line.
