How to read this controller

Functional layout, not anatomy. All 758 functional units are displayed. Lines show only the selected unit's incoming and outgoing mappings; zero weights are hidden. Colours: negative weight, positive weight, fixed link. A line is a stored parameter or mapping, not measured signal flow.

Fast: wave → per-channel sensory KAN; channels 0–95 then cross sensory CfC memory, while 96–191 bypass CfC. L1 mixes all 192 channels into 96 units, followed by L2 with 48 units and the three motor commands. Slow: 48-channel gather → CfC64 → Dense16 → three modulation signals. One-to-one filters do not imply a disconnected controller.

Grey units have no recorded activation. Local recurrent memory, normalization, filtering, fatigue and gates are not drawn as edges. Gear outputs and optional runtime consumers are not represented by the three motor-head units. This is a view of checkpoint weights, not the complete execution graph or proof of learning.
