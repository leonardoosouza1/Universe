# Fly battery results and implementation review

## Decision

The experiment infrastructure is more trustworthy; the biological controller's
behavioral advantage is not established. All nine primary runs and three gain
follow-ups completed. The shuffled map outperformed the real positive-reflex map
on median signed progress in every paired seed. Lower gain reduced saturation
but increased sampled body overlap. Do not promote either change as demonstrated
collision avoidance or learning.

The source protocol is [FLY-BATTERY-2026-09-14.md](FLY-BATTERY-2026-09-14.md).
All simulation outputs are outside the repository:

- Primary: `/media/llm-data/universe-runs/fly-battery-20260914`
- Gain follow-up: `/media/llm-data/universe-runs/fly-gain-check-20260914`
- Spiking visualization: `/media/llm-data/universe-runs/fly-direct-20260914`

Each battery has a frozen executable, map hashes, exact commands, source snapshot,

## Primary results

Each cell is the median signed progress of one population of 40 beings, in metres.
All runs used one generation and 15,000 ticks. The independent unit is the seed.

| Seed | Real, positive reflex | Real, negative reflex | Shuffled, positive reflex |
|---|---:|---:|---:|
| 31 | 63.16 | 62.87 | 147.19 |
| 32 | 38.85 | 46.40 | 201.16 |
| 33 | 53.22 | 33.61 | 136.93 |

Real-positive minus shuffled: −84.03, −162.31, −83.71 m; mean −110.02 m.
Real-positive minus real-negative: +0.29, −7.55, +19.61 m. Reflex sign has no
consistent advantage across these three seeds. There is only one shuffled map;
this experiment cannot distinguish every map-specific confound.

Premature life-budget exhaustion was zero in all runs. All beings exhausted their
budget at the fixed horizon. The old log label `mortality` meant progress below
10 m; current generation/final logs now identify that endpoint correctly. Archived
logs retain their original wording. Progress below 10 m is not physical death.

Mean visible-body coverage was 18.67–30.74%, including invisible samples. This is
above the registered 0.5–15% diagnostic band. Sampled overlap was 22.46–36.16%.
These are retinal sphere/center-distance proxies, not contact impulses. Sampling
can miss intermediate events and bodies share the same interacting world.

## Gain follow-up

The follow-up reused the exact primary executable. Only gain changed from 20 to 1.
It was registered after observing saturation in the first two primary runs.

| Seed | Progress, gain 20 → 1 (m) | Saturated samples, 20 → 1 | Overlap samples, 20 → 1 |
|---|---:|---:|---:|
| 31 | 63.16 → 64.49 | 96.59% → 12.74% | 26.59% → 42.40% |
| 32 | 38.85 → 73.93 | 96.67% → 12.15% | 34.42% → 39.71% |
| 33 | 53.22 → 72.69 | 96.43% → 12.19% | 31.66% → 48.97% |

The signal improvement is consistent. The overlap increase is also consistent.
Progress alone would select a change that worsens the proximity endpoint. This
is a concrete reason to validate the objective before running more evolution.

## Correctness changes

- Starting formation follows track arc length, validates road occupancy and body
  spacing, and records individual initial progress. Random-start flags cannot
  silently override this condition.
- Diagonal whiskers now rotate the body's resident forward vector on the GPU.
  Previously the headless runtime supplied phase zero, leaving them world-aligned.
  The CPU reference also had an incorrect channel order. An independent wall
  fixture checks the corrected frame and channel mapping.
- The full GPU validator now returns failure status when a required section
  fails; its native-brain device limits cover its actual storage bindings.
- A retinal readback now polls after mapping, avoiding a completion deadlock.
- Missing/nonfinite Fly commands invalidate the run before physics. Failed ticks
  no longer become apparently successful partial generations.
- Retina host responsibilities, starting formation, brain selection, telemetry,
  validation support, and browser presentation were extracted into owned modules.
  New code/comments are English; unrelated directories and assets retain paths.

The primary and gain batteries predate the whisker correction and use the legacy
hybrid controller. They are controlled comparisons within that frozen version,
not behavioral validation of the new direct controller or corrected sensors.

## Direct brain and visual demonstration

The [body/brain contract](BRAIN-BODY-INTERFACE.md) identifies resident sensor
buffers and normalized vehicle actions. `--brain-control direct` skips native and
slow neural execution/readback and downstream motor shaping while retaining the
selected Fly readout settings. Native allocations still exist in the constructor.
The body remains a vehicle; FlyWire connectivity does not create legs or wings.

A corrected direct-spiking run completed 512 ticks for eight beings in 71.70 s.
It recorded eight frames for being 0, with 138,639 membrane voltages and spike
counts per frame. All recorded values were finite. Between 12,298 and 12,572
neurons had spikes in the recorded tick windows. Counts are reset each world tick;
the snapshots are not the sum of all activity between 64-tick samples.

Median signed progress was 0 m. The raw brake output was approximately 0.58–0.62
in the sampled being, and minimum model voltage reached approximately −541 mV.
Finite values and CPU/GPU agreement do not validate biological calibration. These
are diagnostic findings requiring controlled input/current and readout calibration,
not a reason to clamp voltages merely to make the picture look plausible.

Both CPU and GPU implement current-based LIF with an unbounded signed synaptic
drive. Their membrane update relaxes toward `v_rest + synaptic_drive`; sustained
inhibition can therefore produce these large negative values without a GPU-only
error. A two-neuron inhibitory fixture with an analytic equilibrium is the next
unit/scale check. Separately, 156 substeps of 0.1 ms advance 15.6 ms per 15.625 ms
world tick, while readout decay uses the full world interval. This loses 12.8 ms
over 512 ticks. A fractional scheduler and trace timing metadata require a paired
change; the current viewer explicitly uses the existing 156-step contract.

`brain-body.html` is a standalone replay of measured frames, body positions,
sensors and commands. Graph layout is organized by cell type, not anatomical
coordinates; only the traced being has activation data. The interface labels
sampling and missing activation explicitly. This run demonstrates integration and
observability, not learned driving or biological equivalence.

## Performance and validation

The primary process monitor collected 994 samples over part of the battery:
median CPU 7.87% of one logical core, global GPU busy 93%, global VRAM 1.52 GB.
These system-wide GPU counters are not per-process attribution or clean kernel
benchmarks. Diagnostics were enabled. GPU runs were sequential.

The spiking run reported 39,936 bytes of per-tick uniform upload in one call and
17,392 bytes/tick of amortized neural readback with full-neuron tracing enabled.
That readback includes diagnostic snapshots and cannot be presented as ordinary
controller traffic. Physics still uploads and reads state every tick. The
documented transfer budget is not evidence of a completely GPU-resident loop.

Commands run successfully on the corrected sources:

```sh
cargo check --release --all-targets
cargo test --release --lib --bin gpu_evolve --bin gpu_validate --bin fly_map_view --test wgsl_validation
FLY_MAP_BENCH=/media/llm-data/datasets/flywire/flymap_783_reflex_v3.bin cargo test --release --lib -- --ignored --test-threads=1 --nocapture
target/release/gpu_validate
BASE_REF=HEAD bash scripts/check_language.sh
```

Regression counts: 446 library, 84 evolution, 3 validator, 8 viewer, 9 WGSL tests
passed; the separately invoked 14 GPU tests all passed. Full-validator perception
matched 1,200 channels within its one-unit criterion. Retina's maximum difference
was one encoded unit; native-brain 100-tick CfC maximum drift was 1.79e−7.
Physics's existing 100-step gate passed, but maximum component differences were
0.141869 m position and 0.133116 m/s velocity. This is not numerical identity.
Logs are `/media/llm-data/scratch/final-{regression,gpu-tests,gpu-validator,all-targets,language}.log`.

## Next scientific gate

First calibrate sensory signs, distances, neural current/voltage scales and action
readout in isolated scenes. Then compare direct Fly, small recurrent baseline,
constant-action baseline, retina-off and multiple shuffled maps on held-out
tracks/seeds with identical budgets. Record actual contacts or explicitly retain
the weaker proximity endpoint. Initial checkpoint credit and warmup movement in
the legacy fitness path remain audit items before using that fitness for learning.

An evolvable graph of mathematical operators is a useful additional controller
candidate once this gate is reliable. Bound graph size and numerical ranges, keep
memory as explicit state, and compare on the same observations/actions. Search
structure between generations; execute the resulting numerical program in GPU
buffers during the lifetime. Do not rewrite the validated physics or introduce an
unrestricted per-being expression interpreter as the first experiment.
