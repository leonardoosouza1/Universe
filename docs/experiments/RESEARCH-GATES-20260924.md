This is engineering validation, followed by exploratory training triage. No population-level effectiveness claim is planned from the short battery.

3. Existing school and traffic GPU fixtures must still pass. Run no CPU simulation tests.

Short service battery: evolutionary seeds 11,12,13; 8 genomes, K=3 starts; 6 generations x 256 ticks, prepared pickup at zero approach distance; fixed bodies, --no-slow in every arm, and no lifetime plasticity. Compare legacy mutation, head-only mutation, and a stateless linear policy. MDE: not estimated from prior variance; this is triage, not confirmatory superiority testing. Stop on malformed artifacts, GPU failure or time budget. Report each seed, first/middle/last, event counts and prepared-state success; independent units are evolutionary seeds, not ticks/replicas.
5. Canary scenarios: one generation each grid/compact/wide with explicit environment seeds, pickup/dropoff witness feasibility, archive/curriculum telemetry. Fixed/random/evolved body mechanisms are verified by expressed fields, not presumed improvement.

Per-run manifest freezes binary/source SHA, command, seed, wall budget, tick ceiling and expected number of records. Failed contracts abort. Shader/unit fixtures are separate from learning. No 256-tick result may claim travel to distant delivery stations or autonomous survival.
